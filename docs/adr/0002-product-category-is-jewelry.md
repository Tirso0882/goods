# The product category is jewelry, imported from China

We chose jewelry for the store, without watches. Demand is growing (Gemius: 32% of online shoppers in 2023, 38% in 2025), it has the best margin outlook of the 15 categories (shipping and payment fees are about 5% of a 350 PLN order), parcels are tiny, and the regulatory burden is medium with no licence. It also suits the agent: products have rich attributes (metal, stone, size, length), and customers ask real questions about ring sizing, nickel allergies, care, gifts and engraving. We will source from Chinese manufacturers, comparing several before negotiating prices. No supplier is chosen yet. [Bamoer](https://bamoer.com/) (925 sterling silver and moissanite, Shenzhen) is the reference example of the kind of product and store we want: its catalog structure, product attributes, size guide, care guide and return policy guide the synthetic catalog and the storefront. The evidence is in the four `product-*-poland.md` reports in `docs/research/`.

## Considered options

| Category | Demand | Competition | Regulation | Margin outlook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jewelry (chosen) | Gemius 38%, up from 32% | Medium to high | Medium | Healthy |
| Sports, padel first (runner-up) | Padel searches about 13x in 5 years | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Home and garden, raised beds first | GUS 12.4%, up from 9.4% | Medium | Medium | Medium |

Padel lost because racket specs and shoe sizes drive returns, the margin is only medium, and its fast growth comes from a small base that could cool off or fill up quickly. Home and garden lost on bulky parcels and strong seasonality (April peak), with few support questions beyond dimensions. We rejected clothing (16% to 30% return rates), beauty and supplements (high regulation, very crowded) and electronics (high regulation, very crowded).

## Consequences

- **We are the importer.** Buying from China makes us the importer and the EU responsible person under GPSR (Art. 11, 16). Listings must name the manufacturer and us, show a photo and identifier, and carry Polish warnings.
- **Hallmarks.** Gold items of 1 g or more and silver items of 5 g or more need Polish or recognised hallmarks before sale (prawo probiercze Art. 4 to 6). Chinese hallmarks are probably not recognised, so heavier silver pieces go through the assay office. Lighter pieces may need a registered maker's mark.
- **Chemical limits.** Every piece must meet the REACH limits on nickel release, cadmium and lead. We need supplier test reports before we list anything.
- **Customs.** The EUR 150 customs relief ended on 1 July 2026. A duty of EUR 3 per item applies until 1 July 2028, plus import VAT.
- **No watches.** Quartz watches and smartwatches would add WEEE, battery and radio-equipment duties.
- **Chinese brands sell direct.** Brands like Bamoer sell to Poland in PLN, with free shipping over $49 and 30-day returns, at about $26 to $52 for most rings, earrings and necklaces. That sets the price ceiling. Wholesale terms must leave room for Allegro's commission (11.5% up to 110 PLN, then 7.5%). Reselling a supplier's brand needs its written permission; selling under our own brand makes us the manufacturer under GPSR (Art. 13).
- **Seasonality.** Demand peaks in December (about 1.7x the low month), so stock and cash flow will be uneven.

The synthetic catalog, FAQ, return policy and evals use jewelry from Setup 1 onwards.
