# Selling online in Poland as a sole trader: product rules by category

Research date: 28 September 2026.

**Scope.** What a Polish sole trader (JDG, jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza) takes on when selling goods online to consumers (B2C) in 15 product categories. It covers EU regulations and directives that apply in Poland, Polish statutes, and what Polish authorities (UOKiK, GIS, BDO) say on their own sites. It does not cover income tax, ZUS, general VAT registration, or selling to other EU countries in depth.

**Disclaimer.** This is not legal advice. It describes what the rules say, based on primary sources as of the date above. Rules change, and how they apply to a given business depends on facts (what you sell, where you buy it, whose brand is on it, volumes). Check with a lawyer or accountant before acting on anything here.

**How sources are cited.** EU law links go to EUR-Lex (ELI links), with article numbers. Polish statutes link to the Sejm's official ELI API (`api.sejm.gov.pl`), which serves the same consolidated texts as ISAP (isap.sejm.gov.pl, the official Polish legal database); I used the API because ISAP pages blocked automated reading. A few Polish government pages are on `gov.pl` subdomains (`biznes.gov.pl`, `parp.gov.pl`, `dziennikustaw.gov.pl`, `senat.gov.pl`). Where I could not confirm something from a primary source, I say so and list it at the end.

## Short summary

- **Every seller now carries GPSR duties.** Since 13 December 2024 every online listing must show the manufacturer, an EU responsible person if the manufacturer is outside the EU, product identification with a picture, and warnings in Polish ([GPSR Art. 19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)). Poland's enforcement act has applied since 3 January 2026 and sets fines of up to PLN 500,000 for breaching Art. 19 ([Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1826, Art. 76](https://dziennikustaw.gov.pl/D2025000182601.pdf)).
- **Your role decides your duties.** If you import from outside the EU you are the importer. If you sell under your own brand you are the manufacturer. Either way you carry far more than a distributor who buys from an EU wholesaler ([GPSR Art. 11, 12, 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Almost every online seller needs a BDO number.** Anyone who puts packaged goods on the Polish market (including shipping boxes) must register in BDO and file a yearly report by 15 March. Below 1 tonne of packaging a year, you are exempt from the product fee if you file the de minimis documents on time ([packaging act Art. 6(3), Art. 7](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/619/text.pdf)).
- **Heaviest categories:** consumer electronics accessories, cosmetics (if you are the responsible person), dietary supplements, toys, household chemicals, DIY tools and car parts. **Lightest:** books and media.
- **Supplements are the sharpest edge.** Selling food online without registering with the sanitary inspection (sanepid) is a criminal offence in Poland ([food safety act Art. 98](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)).
- **Big 2026 to 2027 dates:** PPWR (12 Aug 2026), formaldehyde limits for furniture (6 Aug 2026), PFHxA ban in clothing (10 Oct 2026), EUDR for wood products (30 Dec 2026), Machinery Regulation (20 Jan 2027), battery removability and QR codes (18 Feb 2027), end of the EUR 150 customs relief (1 Jul 2026).

## 1. Duties for every online seller

### General product safety (GPSR) and the Polish enforcement act

The General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988 has applied since 13 December 2024 ([Art. 52](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)). It covers consumer products not fully covered by specific EU safety rules, and fills gaps for products that are.

- **Only safe products** may be placed on the market ([Art. 5](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Online listing information** must be shown clearly: manufacturer's name and postal and email address; the EU responsible person if the manufacturer is outside the EU; product identification including a picture, type and any other identifier; and warnings or safety information in the language of the consumer's country ([Art. 19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Manufacturer duties:** risk analysis and technical documentation kept for 10 years, a type, batch or serial number on the product, name and address on the product, instructions and safety information in Polish ([Art. 9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Importer duties:** check the manufacturer did its job, put the importer's name and address on the product (or packaging or accompanying document), keep storage conditions safe ([Art. 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Distributor duties:** before selling, check that manufacturer and importer labelling and information are present; do not sell a product you believe is unsafe; tell the manufacturer and authorities if a product is dangerous ([Art. 12](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Own brand means manufacturer.** Anyone placing a product on the market under their own name or trademark is treated as the manufacturer ([Art. 13(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **EU responsible person.** A product may not be placed on the market unless an economic operator established in the EU is responsible for it (manufacturer, importer, authorised representative or fulfilment service provider) ([Art. 16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj), referring to [Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 Art. 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj)).
- **Internal safety processes** are required for economic operators ([Art. 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Accidents.** The manufacturer must report accidents causing death or serious harm through the EU Safety Business Gateway. Importers and distributors who learn of an accident must tell the manufacturer ([Art. 20](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj), [Art. 27](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Recalls.** Written recall notices must follow set content; consumers must be offered at least a repair, replacement or refund ([Art. 35 to 37](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- **Polish enforcement act.** The Act of 7 November 2025 on supervision of general product safety ([Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1826](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20250001826)) has applied since 3 January 2026 and repealed the 2003 act. The UOKiK President is the supervisory authority (Art. 6) and the Trade Inspection runs checks (Art. 22). Fines include up to PLN 500,000 for Art. 19 failures (Art. 76), up to PLN 200,000 for a manufacturer that fails Art. 9(7) instructions or accident reporting (Art. 63), and up to PLN 200,000 for a distributor that fails to report a dangerous product or accident (Art. 73) ([official PDF](https://dziennikustaw.gov.pl/D2025000182601.pdf)). PARP (a government agency) confirms the 3 January 2026 start ([parp.gov.pl](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/90288:bezpieczenstwo-produktow-konsumenckich-rozporzadzenie-gpsr-i-nowa-ustawa-o-nadzorze-nad-ogolnym-bezpieczenstwem-produktow)).

### Who you are: manufacturer, importer or distributor

| Your situation | Your role | Main source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You buy from a Polish or EU wholesaler and resell under their brand | Distributor | [GPSR Art. 12](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj) |
| You import from China (or any non-EU country) under the maker's brand | Importer, and usually the EU responsible person | [GPSR Art. 11, 16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj); [2019/1020 Art. 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj) |
| You sell under your own brand or trademark, wherever it was made | Manufacturer | [GPSR Art. 13(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj) |
| You change a product in a way that affects safety | Manufacturer for that part or the whole product | [GPSR Art. 13(2)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj) |

For CE-marked products (toys, electrical goods, PPE, machinery and others), [Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 Art. 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj) requires an EU-based economic operator to hold the EU declaration of conformity, give authorities information, cooperate on corrective action, and have its name and contact details on the product, packaging, parcel or accompanying document. The sector acts use the same pattern: the importer or distributor who sells under its own name takes on the manufacturer's duties (for example [Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC Art. 8](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2009/48/oj); [Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU Art. 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/35/oj)).

Environmental laws use their own definitions. In the Polish WEEE act a "wprowadzający sprzęt" includes a Polish business that sells equipment under its own brand or brings it in from another EU country or from outside the EU ([WEEE act Art. 4 pt 20](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/573/text.html)). In the Batteries Regulation a "producer" includes anyone established in a Member State who first supplies batteries there, including batteries built into products ([Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 Art. 3(47)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).

### Consumer law

- **Pre-contract information** for distance contracts ([ustawa o prawach konsumenta Art. 12](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text.pdf)).
- **14-day right of withdrawal** ([Art. 27](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text.pdf)), with exceptions such as custom-made goods, perishable goods, and sealed goods unsealed after delivery that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons ([Art. 38](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text.pdf)).
- **Conformity with the contract:** the seller is liable for non-conformity that appears within two years of delivery ([Art. 43c](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text.pdf)); remedies are repair or replacement first ([Art. 43d](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text.pdf)).
- **Omnibus price rules:** show price and unit price; whenever you announce a price cut, show the lowest price from the 30 days before the cut ([ustawa o informowaniu o cenach towarów i usług Art. 4](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/168/text.pdf), implementing [Directive (EU) 2019/2161](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/2161/oj)).
- **Polish language:** in consumer trade in Poland, product names, descriptions, instructions, warnings and consumer information must be in Polish; foreign-language text must be accompanied by a Polish version ([ustawa o języku polskim Art. 7, 7a](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/81/text.pdf)).
- **Accessibility (EAA).** The European Accessibility Act applies from 28 June 2025 ([Directive (EU) 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj)). The Polish act excludes services offered by microenterprises, which covers the e-commerce service of most sole traders ([ustawa o zapewnianiu spełniania wymagań dostępności Art. 4 pt 1](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/731/text.pdf)). Product duties (for example for e-readers) still apply to distributors, with lighter rules for microenterprises trading in products ([same act, Art. 3 and the microenterprise clause in the conformity chapter](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/731/text.pdf)).
- **Digital Services Act:** mainly concerns platforms and marketplaces. A seller running its own shop is not a platform ([Regulation (EU) 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj)).
- **Right to repair.** Directive (EU) 2024/1799 had to be transposed by 31 July 2026 ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1799/oj)). Poland has not done this yet. The government draft adds a 12-month extension of seller liability when the consumer chooses repair, and a duty to tell consumers about it ([gov.pl project page](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/projekt-ustawy-o-zmianie-ustawy-o-prawach-konsumenta-oraz-niektorych-innych-ustaw6)). The consolidated consumer act as of 2 September 2026 does not contain these changes ([Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1244](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text.pdf)).
- **Green claims.** Directive (EU) 2024/825 bans unproven generic environmental claims and uncertified sustainability labels, applying from 27 September 2026 ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/825/oj)). Poland's implementing law was passed by the Sejm on 4 September 2026 with entry into force set for 27 September 2027, a year late ([Senate expert opinion](https://www.senat.gov.pl/gfx/senat/pl/senatekspertyzy/8071/plik/825.pdf)). Misleading green claims can already be treated as unfair practices under existing law ([same opinion](https://www.senat.gov.pl/gfx/senat/pl/senatekspertyzy/8071/plik/825.pdf)).
- **Product liability.** The new Product Liability Directive (EU) 2024/2853 must be transposed by 9 December 2026 and covers products placed on the market after that date. Where the manufacturer is outside the EU, the importer or authorised representative (or, failing those, the fulfilment service provider) can be liable ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/2853/oj)). I could not find a Polish implementing act in force.

### Packaging, BDO and the product fee

- **Who is covered.** Anyone who introduces packaged products onto the Polish market, including e-commerce shipping packaging, must register in the BDO register before starting ([biznes.gov.pl, BDO registration](https://www.biznes.gov.pl/pl/portal/ou170); register at [bdo.mos.gov.pl](https://bdo.mos.gov.pl)). The same page lists the registration fee (PLN 200 for microenterprises).
- **Recycling targets and product fee.** Introducers must hit recycling targets or pay a product fee, and must fund public education campaigns ([packaging act Art. 19](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/619/text.pdf)).
- **1 tonne exemption.** If the total packaging you introduced in a year is 1 tonne (1 Mg) or less, the recycling targets, product fee and education campaign duties do not apply ([Art. 6(3)](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/619/text.pdf)), but only if you file the de minimis state aid documents with the marshal's office by 15 March ([Art. 7(1)](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/619/text.pdf)). The annual BDO report is still due.
- **Deposit return system.** Poland's beverage container deposit system runs from 1 October 2025 at the earliest ([packaging act Art. 8 pt 15ba](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/619/text.pdf)). It only matters if you sell drinks in covered containers, which none of the 15 categories centrally involve.
- **Polish EPR reform.** A new packaging act (project UC100) with full extended producer responsibility is in the government legislative process. I could not open the official project page to confirm its current stage, so its status is **unverified**.
- **EU PPWR.** See the packaging section in part 2.

### Selling to other EU countries and importing

- **VAT on distance sales.** Once cross-border B2C sales to other EU countries exceed EUR 10,000 a year, VAT is due in the customer's country, usually paid through OSS ([VAT Directive 2006/112/EC Art. 59c and Title XII Chapter 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/112/oj)). Check with an accountant.
- **Low-value imports.** The EUR 150 customs duty relief ended on 1 July 2026. Until 1 July 2028 a flat EUR 3 duty per item applies to consignments up to EUR 150 sent as distance sales (IOSS) or in postal consignments ([Council Regulation (EU) 2026/382 Art. 1, 2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/382/oj)). This matters if you dropship from China directly to consumers.

## 2. Cross-cutting regimes

### CE marking (harmonised product law)

If a product falls under one of these acts, the manufacturer runs the conformity assessment, writes the EU declaration of conformity and affixes the CE mark. The importer checks this was done and adds its own details. The distributor checks the CE mark, documents and labels.

| Act | Typical products in this report |
| --- | --- |
| [Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/35/oj) | Mains chargers, lamps, electric tools, heated gadgets (50 to 1000 V AC) |
| [EMC Directive 2014/30/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/30/oj) | Almost anything electronic |
| [Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/53/oj) | Bluetooth headphones, smartwatches, wireless mice, remote-control toys |
| [Common charger, Directive (EU) 2022/2380](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2380/oj) | USB-C port for phones, headphones, earbuds, speakers, keyboards, mice, cameras, consoles, e-readers (from 28 Dec 2024) and laptops (from 28 Apr 2026) |
| [RoHS 2011/65/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/65/oj) | All electrical and electronic equipment (hazardous substance limits) |
| [Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/42/oj), replaced by [Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj) on 20 Jan 2027 | Power tools, lawnmowers, e-bikes |
| [PPE Regulation (EU) 2016/425](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/425/oj) | Bike and ski helmets, protective pads, sunglasses |
| [Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2009/48/oj), replaced by [Toy Safety Regulation (EU) 2025/2509](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2509/oj) on 1 Aug 2030 | Toys, some craft kits for children |
| [Gas Appliances Regulation (EU) 2016/426](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/426/oj) | Gas barbecues, patio heaters |
| [Outdoor noise Directive 2000/14/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2000/14/oj) | Lawnmowers, hedge trimmers, leaf blowers (noise marking) |

The Machinery Regulation applies from 20 January 2027 (date as corrected by the [corrigendum of 4 July 2023](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/corrigendum/2023-07-04/oj); see also the [EUR-Lex summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/LSU/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1230)). Machines placed on the market before that date stay under the Directive.

The [Cyber Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj) adds cybersecurity requirements for connected products. Manufacturers' reporting duties apply from 11 September 2026 and the rest from 11 December 2027 ([EUR-Lex summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/LSU/?uri=oj%3AL_202402847)).

### Toys

- Current law: [Directive 2009/48/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2009/48/oj). Manufacturer duties including safety assessment, technical file and CE mark (Art. 4), importer duties (Art. 6), distributor duties (Art. 7), and warnings (Art. 11). Polish text is required through the Polish language act ([Art. 7a](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/81/text.pdf)).
- New law: [Toy Safety Regulation (EU) 2025/2509](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2509/oj), adopted 26 November 2025, published 12 December 2025, in force 1 January 2026 (20 days after publication). It applies from 1 August 2030, and the Directive is repealed on that date ([Art. 56, 59](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2509/oj)). Toys placed on the market under the Directive before 1 August 2030 may still be sold ([Art. 57](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2509/oj)).
- **Digital product passport:** before placing a toy on the market, the manufacturer must create a DPP. It replaces the EU declaration of conformity ([Art. 19, 20](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2509/oj)). This applies from 1 August 2030.

### Cosmetics

- **Responsible person** in the EU for every cosmetic ([Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 Art. 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)). An importer is the responsible person for products it imports; a distributor becomes one if it sells under its own name or changes the product.
- **Safety report** by a qualified safety assessor ([Art. 10](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)), **product information file** ([Art. 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)), **CPNP notification** before placing on the market ([Art. 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)), **labelling** ([Art. 19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)), **claims** ([Art. 20](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)), **serious undesirable effects** reporting ([Art. 23](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)). Distributors must check labelling, language and dates before selling ([Art. 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)).
- **Polish cosmetics act** ([ustawa o produktach kosmetycznych 2018](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2018/2227/text.html)): the PIF may be in Polish or English, but Part B of the safety report must be in Polish (Art. 3); cosmetics sold in Poland must be labelled in Polish (Art. 4); cosmetics may only be sold until their minimum durability date (Art. 5); fines of up to PLN 35,000 for placing on the market without CPNP notification, which also applies to distributors in some cases (Art. 32).
- **Notifying sanepid:** only the **manufacturer** of cosmetics must apply for entry in the register of manufacturing sites, within 30 days of starting ([cosmetics act Art. 6](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2018/2227/text.html); [GIS page](https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/wytwarzanie)). A pure reseller does not.

### Food and dietary supplements

- **Registration with sanepid.** Food businesses, including those selling food at a distance or online, must apply for entry in the register of establishments supervised by the State Sanitary Inspection at least 14 days before starting. Online sellers do not need approval, only registration ([food safety act (UBŻŻ) Art. 61, 63(2) pt 10, 63(3), 64(1)](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf); [biznes.gov.pl guide](https://www.biznes.gov.pl/pl/portal/ou520)).
- **Criminal penalty.** Running an online food business without that registration is punishable by restriction of liberty or up to one year in prison ([UBŻŻ Art. 98](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)).
- **GIS notification** by whoever first places a supplement on the Polish market, with a Polish label template and composition ([UBŻŻ Art. 29](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf); [GIS electronic notification system](https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/elektroniczny-system-powiadomien-powiadomienie-o-wprowadzeniu-po-raz-pierwszy-do-obrotu-suplementy-diety-zywnosc-wzbogacana-zywnosc-dla-okreslonych-grup)).
- **Polish labelling** for all food ([UBŻŻ Art. 48(2)](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)).
- **EU rules:** [Directive 2002/46/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/46/oj) (permitted vitamins and minerals, labelling in Art. 6); [Regulation (EU) 1169/2011](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/oj) (food information; for distance selling, mandatory information must be available before purchase, Art. 14); [Regulation (EC) 1924/2006](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/oj) (only authorised health claims, Art. 10).
- **Supplement advertising.** I found no enacted Polish law in 2024 to 2026 that restricts supplement advertising beyond general food and claims law. The government draft UD 247 amending the food safety act (notification procedure and penalties) was still at government stage, with comments due by 15 September 2026 ([gov.pl, Committee for Digital Affairs page](https://www.gov.pl/web/kdsc/projekt-ustawy-o-zmianie-ustawy-o-bezpieczenstwie-zywnosci-i-zywienia-oraz-ustawy-o-panstwowej-inspekcji-sanitarnej-ud-247)). What the current draft says about advertising is **unverified**.

### Textiles and footwear

- **Textile labelling:** fibre composition on a label or marking, using the listed fibre names ([Regulation (EU) 1007/2011 Art. 5, 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1007/oj)); fibre composition must be available to consumers before purchase, including online ([Art. 16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1007/oj)). The label must be in Polish ([ustawa o języku polskim Art. 7a](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/81/text.pdf)).
- **Footwear labelling:** materials of upper, lining and sole, by pictogram or text ([Directive 94/11/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1994/11/oj)).
- **PFHxA ban:** from 10 October 2026, consumer clothing, accessories and footwear must not contain PFHxA above 25 ppb (or related substances above 1,000 ppb); other consumer textiles follow on 10 October 2027 ([Regulation (EU) 2024/2462, REACH Annex XVII entry 79](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2462/oj)).
- **Destruction ban:** from 19 July 2026 large companies may not destroy unsold clothing and footwear; micro and small enterprises are exempt, and medium ones are covered from 19 July 2030 ([ESPR (EU) 2024/1781 Art. 25(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj); derogations in [Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/296](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/296/oj)).

### Electrical equipment and WEEE

- **EU:** producers must be registered in each country where they sell, including distance sellers ([Directive 2012/19/EU Art. 16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2012/19/oj)); a distance seller into another EU country must appoint an authorised representative there ([Art. 17](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2012/19/oj)).
- **Polish WEEE act** ([consolidated text 2024](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/573/text.html)):
  - A "wprowadzający sprzęt" registers in BDO and puts the BDO number on invoices (Art. 12).
  - It meets its duties itself or through a WEEE recovery organisation under a written contract (Art. 11).
  - It must include information for households on the separate collection ban and hazards (Art. 13) and mark equipment with the crossed-out bin symbol (Art. 14).
  - If it handles collection itself, or has no recovery organisation contract, it must lodge a yearly financial guarantee (Art. 27).
  - **Every distributor, including online sellers:** take back old household equipment of the same type free of charge at the delivery address when delivering new equipment, and publish this information on the website (Art. 37(2), 37(4)).

### Batteries

- **EU Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542** ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj); [consolidated](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02023R1542-20250731)):
  - A "producer" includes the business that first supplies batteries in a Member State, including batteries built into products ([Art. 3(47)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
  - Producer register and extended producer responsibility from 18 August 2025 ([Art. 55, 56](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj); Chapter VIII date in Art. 96).
  - Distributors must take back waste batteries free of charge ([Art. 62](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
  - Labelling with general information: from 18 August 2026 or 18 months after the Commission's labelling implementing act, whichever is later. QR code on all batteries from 18 February 2027 ([Art. 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
  - Portable batteries in products must be readily removable and replaceable by the end user from 18 February 2027 ([Art. 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
  - Due diligence on cobalt, lithium, nickel and graphite is postponed to 18 August 2027 ([Regulation (EU) 2025/1561](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/1561/oj)) and does not apply to operators with turnover under EUR 40 million ([Art. 47](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
- **Polish battery act** ([ustawa o bateriach i akumulatorach, consolidated 2025](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/809/text.pdf)): collection financing (Art. 27), BDO number on documents (Art. 30), a contract with a collector for portable batteries (Art. 32), and for retail sellers of lead-acid car batteries, a deposit fee from consumers who do not hand in an old battery (Art. 54). A new act to align with the EU Regulation (project UC107) was announced but not yet adopted ([gov.pl project page](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/projekt-ustawy-o-bateriach-i-zuzytych-bateriach)).

### EU packaging regulation (PPWR)

- [Regulation (EU) 2025/40](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj) applies from 12 August 2026 ([Art. 71](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj)). Most substantive duties phase in later.
- **PFAS in food-contact packaging** banned above set limits from 12 August 2026 ([Art. 5(5)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj)).
- **Harmonised sorting label** (including on e-commerce packaging) from 12 August 2028 or 24 months after the implementing act, whichever is later ([Art. 12(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj); [Commission notice C/2026/3084](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/3084/oj)).
- **Empty space in e-commerce boxes** at most 50% by 1 January 2030 or 3 years after the method act; sellers who ship in the product's own sales packaging are exempt ([Art. 24](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj)).
- **Producer register reporting** by 1 June for the previous year, with a lighter data set under 10 tonnes ([Art. 44(7), 44(8)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj)). How Poland applies this through BDO depends on the pending national act.

### Chemicals

- **REACH** ([Regulation (EC) 1907/2006](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj)): Annex XVII restrictions apply to every seller. Suppliers of articles containing a substance of very high concern above 0.1% must give recipients safe-use information, and consumers on request within 45 days ([Art. 33](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj)). Recent restrictions: formaldehyde ([2023/1464](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1464/oj)), PFHxA ([2024/2462](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2462/oj)), microplastics including loose plastic glitter ([2023/2055](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2055/oj)).
- **CLP** ([Regulation (EC) 1272/2008](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1272/oj)): hazard labelling (Art. 17), child-resistant fastenings and tactile warnings for some mixtures (Art. 35). Ads for hazardous mixtures that let the public buy without seeing the label must mention the hazard types (Art. 48). Importers and downstream users placing hazardous mixtures on the market must notify poison centres through the ECHA portal and print the UFI code on the label (Art. 45 and Annex VIII). In Poland the appointed body is the Biuro do spraw Substancji Chemicznych, and old national ELDIOM notifications lapsed on 1 January 2025 ([gov.pl/web/chemikalia](https://www.gov.pl/web/chemikalia/zglaszanie-mieszanin-stwarzajacych-zagrozenie)).
- **CLP revision** ([Regulation (EU) 2024/2865](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2865/oj)): new formatting (minimum font sizes), relabelling deadlines, and rules for online advertising and distance sales offers. These were postponed to **1 January 2028** by [Regulation (EU) 2025/2439](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2439/oj). Other parts apply from 1 July 2026 or 1 January 2027 as listed there.
- **Detergents:** [Regulation (EC) 648/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/648/oj) applies now (labelling Art. 11 and Annex VII, including an ingredient data sheet on a website). It is replaced by [Regulation (EU) 2026/405](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/405/oj), in force 23 March 2026 and applying from 23 September 2029 (Art. 37), with a digital product passport and specific online-sale rules.
- **Biocides** ([Regulation (EU) 528/2012](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/528/oj)): a biocidal product (disinfectant, insect repellent, flea treatment) needs an authorisation before it can be sold (Art. 17). "Treated articles" have labelling rules (Art. 58), and advertising must carry set wording (Art. 72). In Poland the competent authority is the President of URPL; biocides and treated articles must be labelled in Polish, and products are entered in the Wykaz Produktów Biobójczych ([ustawa o produktach biobójczych Art. 3, 6(2), 7](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/24/text.pdf)).
- **Polish chemicals act** ([ustawa o substancjach chemicznych i ich mieszaninach](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2022/1816/text.pdf)): sets up the Biuro and national enforcement.

### Deforestation (EUDR)

- [Regulation (EU) 2023/1115](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1115/oj) covers cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya and wood, and listed derived products (including wooden furniture, many wood articles, paper and printed products, tyres and leather; check Annex I for exact codes).
- After [Regulation (EU) 2025/2650](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2650/oj) it applies from **30 December 2026** for large and medium companies and for micro and small companies with products already covered by the old EU Timber Regulation, and from **30 June 2027** for other micro and small companies.
- The operator who first places the product on the EU market (for example a JDG importing wooden furniture from China) files the due diligence statement. Downstream traders mainly keep reference numbers ([Commission simplification review, May 2026](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/74895fc7-47a2-11f1-8095-01aa75ed71a1.0001.02/DOC_1)).

## 3. The 15 categories

Each category also carries the general duties in part 1 (GPSR, consumer law, Polish language, BDO for packaging).

### 3.1 Clothing and footwear

**Burden: medium.** No licence, but fibre labels, Polish text, chemical limits and the new PFHxA ban.

- Fibre composition label using EU fibre names; shown online before purchase ([Regulation (EU) 1007/2011 Art. 5, 14, 16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1007/oj)).
- Footwear material labelling ([Directive 94/11/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1994/11/oj)).
- Labels and care information in Polish ([ustawa o języku polskim Art. 7a](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/81/text.pdf)).
- REACH restrictions, for example azo dyes (entry 43) and chromium VI in leather (entry 47), and SVHC information on request ([REACH Annex XVII, Art. 33](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj)).
- PFHxA ban from 10 October 2026 ([Regulation (EU) 2024/2462](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2462/oj)).
- Children's clothing: cords and drawstrings are a safety issue under GPSR ([GPSR Art. 5, 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- Destruction ban on unsold clothes does not apply to micro and small enterprises ([ESPR Art. 25(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj)).
- Leather goods may fall under EUDR (cattle) from 30 June 2027 for micro and small firms ([Regulation (EU) 2025/2650](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2650/oj)).

### 3.2 Home and garden

**Burden: medium.** Very broad. Kitchenware brings food-contact rules; powered garden tools bring machinery and electrical rules; garden chemicals can bring authorisations.

- Kitchenware and food containers: must not transfer harmful substances to food, need "for food contact" wording or the glass-and-fork symbol, declaration of compliance and traceability ([Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 Art. 3, 15, 16, 17](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/1935/oj)); plastics also need a declaration of compliance ([Regulation (EU) 10/2011 Art. 15](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/10/oj)). Polish labelling required ([UBŻŻ Art. 55](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)). Food-contact businesses are also covered by sanepid registration ([UBŻŻ Art. 61 pt 4, 63(2) pt 11](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)).
- Paper food-contact items: PFHxA ban from 10 October 2026 ([2024/2462](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2462/oj)).
- Electric garden tools and appliances: CE under LVD, EMC, Machinery ([2014/35/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/35/oj), [2014/30/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/30/oj), [2006/42/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/42/oj)), outdoor noise marking ([2000/14/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2000/14/oj)), WEEE and batteries (part 2).
- Gas barbecues and heaters: [Gas Appliances Regulation (EU) 2016/426](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/426/oj).
- Cleaning products, disinfectants, insect sprays: see household chemicals (3.13).
- Charcoal and wooden items may fall under EUDR ([2023/1115](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1115/oj)).
- Plant protection products and fertilisers have their own authorisation and sales rules that I did not research (**unverified**).

### 3.3 Furniture and home decor

**Burden: medium.** Safety (stability), the new formaldehyde limit, and EUDR for wood from the end of 2026.

- General safety, including tip-over risk ([GPSR Art. 5, 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- Formaldehyde emissions from furniture and wood-based articles at most 0.062 mg/m³, other articles 0.080 mg/m³, for articles placed on the market after 6 August 2026 ([Regulation (EU) 2023/1464, REACH entry 77](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1464/oj)).
- EUDR for wooden furniture: from 30 December 2026, including for micro and small firms, because wooden furniture was already covered by the old Timber Regulation ([Regulation (EU) 2025/2650](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2650/oj); [Access2Markets note](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/news/delay-until-december-2026-and-other-developments-implementation-eudr-regulation)). If you import it, you file due diligence statements.
- Lamps and lighting: LVD, EMC, RoHS, WEEE (part 2).
- Candles, decor with batteries (LED), textiles: GPSR, batteries, textile labelling as relevant.

### 3.4 Consumer electronics accessories

**Burden: high.** CE marking under several acts, WEEE and battery producer duties, USB-C rules, and soon cybersecurity.

- CE marking and declaration of conformity under LVD, EMC, RED and RoHS as applicable ([2014/35/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/35/oj), [2014/30/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/30/oj), [2014/53/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/53/oj), [2011/65/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/65/oj)); an EU responsible person ([2019/1020 Art. 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj)).
- USB-C for headphones, earbuds, speakers, keyboards, mice and similar from 28 December 2024; laptops from 28 April 2026 ([Directive (EU) 2022/2380](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2380/oj); [Commission guidance](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2024/2997)).
- WEEE: BDO registration, recovery organisation contract, marking, household information, possibly a financial guarantee, free take-back on delivery ([Polish WEEE act Art. 11 to 14, 27, 37](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/573/text.html)).
- Batteries (power banks, earbuds): producer registration and EPR, labelling, take-back ([Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 Art. 13, 55, 56, 62](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj); [Polish battery act](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/809/text.pdf)); removability from 18 February 2027 ([Art. 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
- Connected products: Cyber Resilience Act reporting from 11 September 2026, full application 11 December 2027 ([Regulation (EU) 2024/2847](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj)).
- Cables and chargers without electronics can still fall under LVD or RoHS depending on voltage and content ([2014/35/EU Art. 1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/35/oj)).

### 3.5 Beauty and cosmetics

**Burden: high** if you import or sell under your own brand; **medium** if you only resell EU products under the maker's brand.

- Responsible person, safety report, PIF, CPNP, labelling ([Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 Art. 4, 10, 11, 13, 19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)).
- Distributor checks before selling ([Art. 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/1223/oj)).
- Polish labels; Part B of the safety report in Polish; sell only within the durability date; fines up to PLN 35,000 for missing CPNP notification ([Polish cosmetics act Art. 3, 4, 5, 32](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2018/2227/text.html)).
- Manufacturing (including filling or repackaging) requires entry in the sanepid register of manufacturing sites within 30 days ([Art. 6](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2018/2227/text.html); [GIS](https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/wytwarzanie)).
- Microplastics: rinse-off cosmetics with microplastics banned from 17 October 2027, leave-on from 17 October 2029, make-up, lip and nail products from 17 October 2035 with labelling from 2031 ([Regulation (EU) 2023/2055, entry 78](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2055/oj)).
- PFHxA banned in cosmetics from 10 October 2026 ([2024/2462](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2462/oj)).
- Withdrawal right: unsealed hygiene products may be excluded ([consumer act Art. 38 pt 5](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text.pdf)).

### 3.6 Health and dietary supplements

**Burden: high.** Sanepid registration before you start (with a criminal penalty for skipping it), GIS notification per product if you are first on the Polish market, and strict labelling and claims rules.

- Register with the district sanitary inspector at least 14 days before starting; online sellers need registration, not approval ([UBŻŻ Art. 63(2) pt 10, 63(3), 64(1)](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)).
- Online food sales without registration are a crime ([UBŻŻ Art. 98](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)).
- Notify GIS before first placing each supplement on the Polish market ([UBŻŻ Art. 29](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf); [GIS](https://www.gov.pl/web/gis/elektroniczny-system-powiadomien-powiadomienie-o-wprowadzeniu-po-raz-pierwszy-do-obrotu-suplementy-diety-zywnosc-wzbogacana-zywnosc-dla-okreslonych-grup)).
- Polish labelling ([UBŻŻ Art. 48(2)](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)); supplement labelling ([Directive 2002/46/EC Art. 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/46/oj)); online pre-purchase food information ([Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 Art. 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/oj)).
- Only authorised health claims ([Regulation (EC) 1924/2006 Art. 10](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/oj)).
- Pending: draft UD 247 on notification procedure ([gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/kdsc/projekt-ustawy-o-zmianie-ustawy-o-bezpieczenstwie-zywnosci-i-zywienia-oraz-ustawy-o-panstwowej-inspekcji-sanitarnej-ud-247)).
- Medicinal products are a separate regime (pharmaceutical law) and are not covered here.

### 3.7 Sports and outdoor

**Burden: medium.** Most gear is GPSR only, but helmets and protective gear are PPE, and e-bikes bring machinery and battery rules.

- Helmets, protective pads, sunglasses: CE marking under PPE rules ([Regulation (EU) 2016/425](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/425/oj)).
- Bicycles: GPSR ([2023/988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)). Polish equipment rules for bicycles used on public roads are in the vehicle technical conditions regulation ([consolidated text Dz.U. 2024 poz. 502](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/502/text.pdf)); I did not confirm the exact provisions (**unverified**).
- E-bikes: Machinery rules (Machinery Regulation from 20 January 2027), EMC, and LMT battery rules including removability by an independent professional from 18 February 2027 ([2023/1230](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj); [2023/1542 Art. 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
- Waterproof outdoor clothing: PFHxA ban from 10 October 2026 ([2024/2462](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2462/oj)).
- Fitness trackers and electronic gear: see 3.4.

### 3.8 Toys and baby products

**Burden: high.** Toys need CE marking, a technical file, warnings and chemical limits; baby products bring food-contact and safety standard issues.

- Toys: safety assessment, technical file, EU declaration of conformity, CE mark (manufacturer); checks by importers and distributors; warnings ([Directive 2009/48/EC Art. 4, 6, 7, 11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2009/48/oj)); EU responsible person ([2019/1020 Art. 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj)).
- Phthalates in toys and childcare articles restricted ([REACH Annex XVII entries 51, 52](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj)).
- New Toy Safety Regulation from 1 August 2030 with a digital product passport ([Regulation (EU) 2025/2509 Art. 19, 59](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2509/oj)).
- Childcare articles (cots, prams, high chairs, dummies): no single EU law; they fall under GPSR, where products meeting European standards referenced in the Official Journal are presumed safe ([GPSR Art. 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)).
- Baby bottles, cups, plates: food-contact rules ([1935/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/1935/oj), [10/2011](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/10/oj)).
- Toys with electronics or batteries: WEEE and batteries (part 2).
- Polish text for warnings and instructions ([ustawa o języku polskim Art. 7a](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/81/text.pdf)).
- Baby food and infant formula are food with extra rules, including GIS notification for some types ([UBŻŻ Art. 29(1) pt 1](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1448/text.pdf)).

### 3.9 Pet supplies

**Burden: medium.** Pet food is feed law with Veterinary Inspection registration; flea and tick products are biocides or veterinary medicines; accessories are GPSR.

- Pet food labelling and marketing ([Regulation (EC) 767/2009](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/767/oj)).
- Feed businesses must be registered ([Regulation (EC) 183/2005 Art. 9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2005/183/oj)). In Poland, apply to the district veterinary officer at least 30 days before starting ([ustawa o paszach Art. 10, 11](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/320/text.pdf)). Whether a pure online reseller of pre-packed pet food must register is a question for a lawyer.
- Flea collars and sprays: either a biocidal product needing authorisation ([528/2012 Art. 17](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/528/oj); Polish URPL, [biocides act Art. 3](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/24/text.pdf)) or a veterinary medicinal product needing a marketing authorisation ([Regulation (EU) 2019/6 Art. 5](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/6/oj)). Online retail of veterinary medicines is limited to persons authorised under national law ([Art. 104](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/6/oj)).
- Pet toys, beds, leads: GPSR ([2023/988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)). Electronic items (GPS collars, automatic feeders): see 3.4.

### 3.10 Car parts and accessories

**Burden: high.** Type-approval marks for many parts, tyre labels, extra producer duties for tyres and oils, and deposit rules for car batteries.

- Replacement parts that affect safety or emissions often need type approval (E-mark) under UN regulations listed in [Regulation (EU) 2018/858 Annex II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/858/oj). Parts that may pose a serious risk to essential systems need an authorisation ([Art. 55](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/858/oj)).
- Tyres: EU tyre label to be shown, including in online offers ([Regulation (EU) 2020/740](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2020/740/oj), supplier and dealer duties).
- Tyres and lubricating oils: recovery and recycling duties or a product fee, and BDO registration ([ustawa o obowiązkach przedsiębiorców w zakresie gospodarowania niektórymi odpadami Art. 3, 4, 12 and Annex 4a](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/433/text.pdf); [biznes.gov.pl BDO page](https://www.biznes.gov.pl/pl/portal/ou170)).
- Car batteries: EU rules for SLI batteries ([2023/1542 Art. 61](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)); in Poland a retail seller must collect a deposit fee from a consumer who does not hand in an old battery ([Polish battery act Art. 54](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/809/text.pdf)).
- Oils, coolants, screen wash: CLP labelling, UFI/PCN, child-resistant closures where required ([CLP Art. 17, 35, 45](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1272/oj)).
- Tyres (rubber) may fall under EUDR from 30 June 2027 for micro and small firms ([2025/2650](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2650/oj)).
- Electronic accessories (dash cams, chargers): see 3.4.

### 3.11 Books and media

**Burden: low.** No product-specific licence. Mainly a VAT question plus general duties.

- Printed books and e-books are taxed at 5% VAT ([VAT act Art. 41(2a), Annex 10](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/775/text.pdf)). I did not find music CDs, films or games in Annex 10, so the standard rate appears to apply (confirm with an accountant).
- I found no Polish fixed book price law in force (the Sejm database search returned no such act).
- PEGI age ratings for games: I found no Polish or EU primary source that makes PEGI mandatory, so I make no claim.
- Printed products are listed in EUDR; for micro and small firms from 30 June 2027 ([2025/2650](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2650/oj)). Whether a July 2026 Commission delegated act changes this is **unverified**.
- Packaging and BDO still apply (part 1).

### 3.12 DIY and tools

**Burden: high.** Power tools bring machinery, electrical, battery and WEEE duties; paints and adhesives bring chemical labelling and VOC limits.

- Power tools: CE under Machinery (Machinery Regulation from 20 January 2027), LVD, EMC, RoHS ([2006/42/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/42/oj), [2023/1230](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj), [2014/35/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/35/oj), [2014/30/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/30/oj), [2011/65/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/65/oj)).
- Cordless tools: battery producer duties and removability from 18 February 2027 ([2023/1542](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)); WEEE ([Polish WEEE act](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/573/text.html)).
- Paints and varnishes: VOC limits and labelling ([Directive 2004/42/EC Art. 3, 4 and Annex II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2004/42/oj)).
- Paints, glues, solvents: CLP, UFI/PCN, and advertising hazard statements ([CLP Art. 17, 45, 48](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1272/oj)); REACH restrictions such as diisocyanates (entry 74).
- Hand tools: GPSR only.

### 3.13 Household chemicals

**Burden: high.** Hazard labelling, poison centre notifications, detergent rules, biocide authorisations and Polish labels.

- CLP classification and labelling, child-resistant fastenings, hazard wording in ads ([CLP Art. 17, 35, 48](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1272/oj)); poison centre notification and UFI if you are the importer or you rebrand ([Art. 45, Annex VIII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1272/oj); [Biuro, gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/chemikalia/zglaszanie-mieszanin-stwarzajacych-zagrozenie)).
- New CLP formatting and distance-sales rules from 1 January 2028 ([2025/2439](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2439/oj)).
- Detergent labelling and ingredient data sheet ([648/2004 Art. 11, Annex VII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/648/oj)); new regulation from 23 September 2029 ([2026/405](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/405/oj)).
- Disinfectants and insecticides are biocides: authorisation, entry in the Polish list, Polish labels ([528/2012 Art. 17, 72](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/528/oj); [Polish biocides act Art. 3, 6, 7](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/24/text.pdf)).
- Safety data sheets for hazardous mixtures sold to professionals ([REACH Art. 31](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj)).
- Microplastics in detergents, waxes and polishes are restricted with transition periods ([2023/2055](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2055/oj)).

### 3.14 Jewelry and watches

**Burden: medium.** Precious metal items above small weights need Polish or recognised hallmarks; all jewelry has chemical limits; electronic watches bring WEEE and battery duties.

- Precious metal items may only be sold with Polish hallmarks, hallmarks recognised under international agreements, hallmarks from another EU/EFTA country or Turkey, or an assay certificate ([prawo probiercze Art. 4, 5](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/536/text.pdf)). Items with less than 1 g of gold or platinum alloy or less than 5 g of silver alloy are exempt ([Art. 6(1)](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/536/text.pdf)). Items are submitted to the assay office for testing and hallmarking ([Art. 12](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/536/text.pdf)).
- Maker's mark: items newly made in Poland, and exempt light items made abroad, must carry a maker's mark registered with the regional assay office ([Art. 18, 19, 20](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/536/text.pdf)). Who exactly must register for imported items is a question for a lawyer.
- REACH limits: nickel release (entry 27), cadmium (entry 23), lead (entry 63) in jewelry ([REACH Annex XVII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj)).
- Quartz watches and smartwatches: WEEE ([Polish WEEE act](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/573/text.html)); button cell and built-in batteries ([2023/1542](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj)).
- Smartwatches: RED, RoHS, CE ([2014/53/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/53/oj)); not in the USB-C list ([Directive (EU) 2022/2380 Annex Ia](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2380/oj)); Cyber Resilience Act from 2026 and 2027 ([2024/2847](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj)).
- Leather straps: chromium VI limit ([REACH entry 47](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj)).

### 3.15 Hobby and craft supplies

**Burden: medium.** Most items are GPSR only, but paints, resins and glues bring CLP, loose plastic glitter is banned, and kits aimed at children can count as toys.

- Loose plastic glitter for crafts has been banned since 17 October 2023 ([Regulation (EU) 2023/2055, entry 78](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2055/oj); [Commission explainer](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/chemicals/reach/restrictions/commission-regulation-eu-20232055-restriction-microplastics-intentionally-added-products_en)).
- Epoxy resins, spray paints, solvents, adhesives: CLP, UFI/PCN, child-resistant closures, hazard wording in ads ([CLP Art. 17, 35, 45, 48](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1272/oj)).
- Craft kits intended for children under 14 can be toys ([Directive 2009/48/EC Art. 2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2009/48/oj)).
- Electric tools (glue guns, soldering irons, sewing machines): LVD, EMC, WEEE (part 2).
- Yarn and fabric: textile labelling ([1007/2011](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1007/oj)).

## 6. Questions to take to a lawyer or accountant

1. If I sell white-label products from a Chinese factory with my brand on them, am I the manufacturer for GPSR, CE marking, WEEE and batteries? What documents must I hold myself?
2. If I dropship from China directly to Polish consumers, who is the importer and the EU responsible person? Does the EUR 3 per item duty (from 1 July 2026) apply to my model, and should I use IOSS?
3. Will my total packaging stay under 1 tonne a year? How do I weigh shipping boxes and fillers for the BDO report, and how do I file the de minimis documents by 15 March?
4. Do I need BDO registration and a WEEE or battery recovery organisation if I only resell items bought from a Polish wholesaler? What if some are bought from an EU seller in Germany?
5. For electronics: do I need a financial guarantee under the WEEE act, and how do I handle the free take-back on delivery?
6. For supplements: does my storage location (home, warehouse, fulfilment centre) change the sanepid registration? If my supplier already notified GIS, do I still need to?
7. For cosmetics: if I import from Korea or the US, who can act as my safety assessor, and can I be the responsible person as a JDG?
8. For pet food: does an online-only reseller of pre-packed pet food need Veterinary Inspection registration?
9. For jewelry: do imported silver items above 5 g need Polish hallmarks if they carry marks from their country of origin? Do I need a maker's mark as an importer?
10. For wooden furniture and decor: am I an EUDR "operator" from 30 December 2026, and what does my due diligence statement need?
11. When do I cross the EUR 10,000 threshold for EU distance sales VAT, and do I need OSS?
12. Which VAT rate applies to each product (books 5%, games and media, baby products, supplements)?
13. Does my shop fall under the accessibility act, or am I covered by the microenterprise exemption for services?
14. What do my terms, returns policy and price-reduction displays need to say under the consumer act and the price information act?
15. When Poland transposes right to repair and product liability, what changes for my returns and liability?
